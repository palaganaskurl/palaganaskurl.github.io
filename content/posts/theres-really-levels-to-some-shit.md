There’s really levels to some shit.

Some people just naturally excel at certain things. You can see it almost immediately—the way they think, create, learn, or perform just feels different. They seem to have an instinct for it that other people have to work years to develop.

And then there are things where, no matter how hard you try, you might never be particularly good at them.

That doesn’t mean effort is useless. Hard work can take you a long way, and you can absolutely improve at almost anything. But I think there’s a point where you have to acknowledge that people have different natural abilities, and some people simply have a head start in certain areas.

There really are levels to this shit.

Sometimes, it’s not about how hard you’re willing to work. Sometimes, someone else is just built different for that specific thing.
